Choose how to play. Camera tracks both hands; mouse works without a camera.

How to play

  • 01 Move a puppetEach hand controls its matching puppet. Your palm moves the body; tilt to lean.
  • 02 Guide the armsUse your thumb and index finger to move the arm rods.
  • 03 Make it danceRaise your little finger for kiprahan. Move closer to enlarge the shadow.
